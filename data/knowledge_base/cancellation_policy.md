# Cancellation Policy

## Before Restaurant Acceptance
Orders can be cancelled free of charge if the restaurant has not yet confirmed the order. The status must be PLACED for a free cancellation.

## After Restaurant Acceptance
Once the restaurant has accepted and confirmed your order (status: CONFIRMED), cancellation is subject to a cancellation fee of ₹50 or 10% of the order total, whichever is less. This compensates the restaurant for preparation that may have already begun.

## During Preparation
If the restaurant has started preparing your food (status: PREPARING), cancellation may not be possible. A cancellation request will be sent to the restaurant, and approval depends on how far along the preparation is. If approved, a cancellation fee of 25% of the order total applies.

## After Dispatch
Orders that are already out for delivery (status: OUT_FOR_DELIVERY or READY) cannot be cancelled. Please refuse the delivery if needed, and contact support for a refund evaluation.

## Refund Timeline for Cancellations
- Cancellation before confirmation: Full refund within 2-3 business days
- Cancellation after confirmation: Refund minus cancellation fee within 3-5 business days
- Cancellation during preparation (if approved): Refund minus 25% fee within 5-7 business days

## Repeated Cancellations
Customers who cancel more than 5 orders in a 30-day period may have their cancellation privileges temporarily restricted. A warning will be issued after the 3rd cancellation.
