# Delivery Policy

## Delivery Hours
We operate from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM daily, including weekends and holidays. Some restaurants may have different operating hours.

## Delivery Zones
Delivery is available within a 15 km radius of partner restaurants. Areas outside this radius may have limited availability or extended delivery times.

## Delivery Time Estimates
- Standard delivery: 30-45 minutes
- Peak hours (12-2 PM, 7-10 PM): 40-60 minutes
- Adverse weather conditions: Additional 15-30 minutes may apply

The estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown in your order is calculated based on restaurant preparation time, distance, and current demand. ETAs are estimates and not guarantees.

## Contactless Delivery
All deliveries default to contactless delivery. The delivery partner will leave the order at your doorstep and notify you. You can opt for hand-to-hand delivery in the app settings.

## Delivery Issues
- **Late delivery**: If your order arrives more than 15 minutes after the maximum ETA, you are eligible for delivery fee waiver or credits.
- **Wrong address**: If the order cannot be delivered due to an incorrect address, the delivery partner will attempt to contact you. If unreachable for 10 minutes, the order may be marked as undeliverable.
- **Damaged packaging**: Report damaged packaging immediately through the support agent. Photo evidence speeds up the resolution.

## Delivery Partner Conduct
Our delivery partners follow a strict code of conduct. Report any unprofessional behavior through the support agent, and it will be addressed within 24 hours.
