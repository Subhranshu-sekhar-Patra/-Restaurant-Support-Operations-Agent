# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?
You can track your order by providing your order code (e.g., ORD-1005) to our support agent. The agent will show you the current status, estimated delivery time, and restaurant details.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, UPI, net banking, and wallet payments (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay). Cash on delivery is available for orders under ₹2000.

## How long does delivery take?
Delivery times vary by restaurant and location. Typical delivery takes 30-45 minutes. You can check the ETA for your specific order using the order tracking feature.

## Can I modify my order after placing it?
You can modify your order only if the restaurant has not yet started preparing it (status: PLACED or CONFIRMED). Once preparation begins, modifications are no longer possible. Contact support immediately if you need changes.

## What if my order is late?
If your order is significantly delayed beyond the estimated delivery time, please contact our support agent. We may offer compensation in the form of credits or a partial refund depending on the delay duration.

## What is the refund window?
Refunds must be requested within 5 days of order delivery. This may differ from newer policies - please check the refund policy document for the most current information.

## How do I contact support?
You can chat with our AI support agent anytime. For issues the agent cannot resolve, a support ticket will be created and a human agent will follow up within 24 hours.
