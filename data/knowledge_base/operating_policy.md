# Operating Policy

## Service Hours
Our platform operates 24/7 for order placement. Restaurant availability varies:
- Most restaurants: 8:00 AM - 11:30 PM
- Late night restaurants: Until 2:00 AM
- Breakfast spots: 6:00 AM - 11:00 AM

## Customer Support Hours
- AI Support Agent: Available 24/7
- Human Support: 8:00 AM - 12:00 AM (midnight) IST
- Priority support for HIGH tickets: Within 2 hours
- Standard ticket response: Within 24 hours

## Order Limits
- Maximum order value: ₹10,000
- Minimum order value: ₹99 (varies by restaurant)
- Maximum items per order: 25
- Maximum concurrent orders per customer: 3

## Account Policies
- One account per phone number/email
- Accounts sharing detected will be suspended
- Fraudulent orders result in permanent ban
- Inactive accounts are archived after 12 months

## Quality Assurance
- All partner restaurants undergo quality verification
- Regular hygiene inspections are conducted
- Customer feedback is monitored and actioned
- Restaurants with ratings below 3.0 are placed on review

## Data Privacy
- Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Order history is retained for 24 months
- Personal information is never shared with third parties without consent
- Customers can request data deletion through support
