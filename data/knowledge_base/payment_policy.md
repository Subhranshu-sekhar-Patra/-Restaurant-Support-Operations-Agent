# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay
- Debit Cards: All major banks
- UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM
- Net Banking: Available for all major banks
- Wallets: Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay
- Cash on Delivery: Available for orders under ₹2000

## Payment Processing
Payments are processed at the time of order placement. Your payment method is charged immediately when you confirm your order. A payment confirmation is sent via email and in-app notification.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails during order placement:
1. The order will not be confirmed
2. Any amount deducted will be automatically refunded within 5-7 business days
3. You may retry with the same or different payment method
4. If the amount was deducted but the order shows as FAILED, please create a support ticket immediately

## Payment Deducted But Order Failed
This is a known scenario that can occur due to:
- Network issues between our payment processor and order system
- Temporary system glitches
- Bank processing delays

In this case:
- The order status will show as FAILED with payment_status DEDUCTED_NOT_CONFIRMED
- An automatic refund is initiated within 24 hours
- If not refunded within 7 business days, contact support with your order code
- These cases are treated as HIGH priority

## Double Charges
If you see a double charge:
1. Wait 24 hours - one charge may be a temporary hold
2. If both charges persist, contact support with your order code
3. The duplicate charge will be refunded within 3-5 business days

## Promotional Credits and Discounts
- Promotional credits are applied before payment processing
- Credits expire 30 days after issuance unless otherwise stated
- Credits cannot be combined with other promotions unless specified
- Refunds for orders paid with credits are issued as credits, not cash
