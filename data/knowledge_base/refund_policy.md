# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
Refunds are available for the following situations:
- Order not delivered within 60 minutes of the maximum ETA
- Wrong items received
- Missing items from the order
- Food quality issues (spoiled, cold, or improperly prepared)
- Payment deducted but order failed or was cancelled by the system

## Refund Window
Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the order delivery date. Requests after this window will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis but are not guaranteed.

## Refund Process
1. Contact our support agent or create a support ticket describing the issue
2. Provide your order code and details about the problem
3. For food quality issues, a photo may be requested (not required for processing)
4. Our team will review the request within 24 hours
5. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method

## Refund Amounts
- Missing items: Refund for the specific items missing
- Wrong items: Full order refund or replacement at customer's choice
- Quality issues: Full or partial refund based on severity
- Delivery failure: Full refund including delivery charges
- Payment deducted but order failed: Full refund processed automatically within 5-7 business days

## Refund Timeline
- UPI/Wallet: 1-2 business days
- Credit/Debit card: 5-7 business days
- Net banking: 3-5 business days

## Non-Refundable Cases
- Order consumed and complaint raised after consumption without evidence
- Customer not available at delivery location (no-show)
- Incorrect delivery address provided by the customer
- Orders cancelled after preparation has started (cancellation fee applies)
